I left Nigeria just a decade ago to find my voice and to hone my musical skills and I am staging these homecoming concert tours in to share and implement my experiences and vision to make significant impact on the Nigerian art music community.

These concerts will showcase various facets of my musical and holistic inclinations including as a singer, conductor, educator and composer premiering some of my compositional works.

I want to build something palpable here in Lagos. World-class training for Nigerian talents, especially youths from underserved background and low-income communities giving them access to equitable music education with an end goal of fostering creative discipline and leadership through the performing arts.

Tune in to my new "Resonance" podcast channel to learn about ongoing discussions, topics and talking points, ideas and plans engaged with my musical friends and collegiates towards facilitating better art music experiences in Nigeria. Be part of the conversation!

The "Resonance" Podcast launches its debut episode on Tuesday the 10th of March on Spotify and YouTube, or on my website.

I truly hope that you are excited to be part of the Roots & Resonance Tour and I am equally thrilled and cannot wait to see you.
